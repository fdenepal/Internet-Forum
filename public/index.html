01WHY THIS FORUM, WHY NOW

Nepal's internet
is at a turning point.

New data protection law, a pending cybersecurity bill, data centre directives, a fast-growing digital economy, and the aftermath of the September 2025 social media shutdown have made internet and digital policy a matter of daily national concern—not a niche technical debate.

NIF is Nepal's own open, multistakeholder forum. Modelled on the national and regional Internet Governance Forum process, it brings government, industry, civil society, the technical community, media, academia, and youth together as equals to shape policy—and carry Nepal's voice into APrIGF and the global Internet Governance Forum.

02THE CONVERSATIONS

Four tracks.
One shared future.

Where infrastructure meets rights, and resilience meets responsibility.

01CONNECTIVITY

Access, Infrastructure & Future Connectivity

Nepal's rural and mountain communities still lag in broadband and affordability. Reliance on a handful of transit links left the network exposed in September 2025. How do we close the gap and shape what's next: spectrum, backbone investment, satellite and next-generation access?

Explore this track ↗
02INTELLIGENCE

Data Centres, Digital Transformation & AI Governance

Nepal's hydropower surplus is drawing data-centre investment while Digital Nepal and AI enter courts, classrooms and public administration ahead of a regulatory framework. How do we build data and AI infrastructure that is viable, trustworthy and governed on our own terms?

Explore this track ↗
03DIGITAL RIGHTS

Digital Platform Governance & Trust

The September 2025 social media shutdown, and the protests that reversed it, exposed how unsettled Nepal's platform governance remains. Work through redesigning social media regulation: balancing accountability against free expression in the pending IT and Cybersecurity Bill.

Explore this track ↗
04RESILIENCE

Disaster & Emergency Communication Infrastructure

Flash floods across Nuwakot, Rasuwa, Dhading and Chitwan in August 2026 knocked out mobile networks and power when coordination mattered most. Examine resilient emergency communications, early-warning networks and interoperable infrastructure for response and recovery.

Explore this track ↗
